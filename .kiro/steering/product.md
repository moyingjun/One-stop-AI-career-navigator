# Product Overview

One-stop AI Career Navigator (一站式AI职业生涯导航员) — an AI-powered career guidance platform for IT job seekers in the Chinese market.

## Core Features

- **Resume Diagnosis**: Upload and AI-analyze resumes (PDF/DOCX), providing structured feedback and scoring
- **Mock Interview**: AI-driven interview simulation with streaming responses
- **Career Planning**: Personalized career path recommendations based on user profile
- **Knowledge Base (RAG)**: System knowledge store with domain expertise (张雪峰-style career advice)
- **History & Saved Chats**: Persistent record of all AI interactions with save/bookmark capability
- **Dashboard**: Central hub showing user profile summary and quick access to all features

## Target Users

Chinese IT professionals and job seekers looking for career guidance, resume improvement, and interview preparation.

## Key Design Principles

- Conversational AI interactions with streaming (SSE) responses
- Mobile-friendly with responsive design
- Guest-accessible landing with setup gate for personalized features
- All AI responses in Chinese with professional career-coaching tone
